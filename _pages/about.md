## About Me

I am Xiaobing Du (杜肖兵). My research interests span human–AI collaboration, large language model agents, multimodal learning, and affective computing. I am interested in combining learning-based methods with symbolic reasoning and cognitive models to support reliable reasoning, decision-making, and human–AI collaboration.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2022, and my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Shandong University in 2016.

My industry experience includes working as a Senior Engineer at Huawei's Central Software Institute, 2012 Laboratories. My work has covered multimodal application retrieval, travel planning agent development, agent-based code generation, cloud incident diagnosis, and cognitive modeling for human–task matching.

## Research Interests

- **LLM agents and neuro-symbolic reasoning:** integrating language models with tools, optimization solvers, memory, and reflection to address complex tasks.
- **Multimodal learning and retrieval:** learning from text and images, improving semantic retrieval, and distilling large models for efficient inference.
- **Human–AI collaboration:** modeling human capabilities and task complexity to support task allocation and reduce operational risk.
- **Affective computing:** recognizing emotions from EEG signals through deep learning, multi-label classification, and emotion distribution learning.

## Selected Research and Industry Projects

### Multimodal Retrieval for App Search

Worked on combining application descriptions and screenshots to improve semantic understanding and long-tail query retrieval. Used knowledge distillation to transfer multimodal model capabilities to a smaller model for online retrieval.

### Neuro-Symbolic Travel Planning

Developed a travel-planning agent that combines language-model-based intent understanding and tool use with integer programming. The approach incorporates constraints such as opening hours, travel distances, and budgets into itinerary planning.

### Agents for Code Generation and Cloud Operations

Worked on Ascend C operator generation using reinforcement learning, tool interaction, reflection, and retrieval of previous experience. Also developed an SRE copilot for cloud incident diagnosis and resolution support using retrieval-augmented generation and monitoring tools.

### Cognitive Modeling for Human–Task Matching

Combined cognitive models, domain knowledge, and graph learning to assess personnel competency and match people to cloud operation tasks. Investigated task complexity through situation-awareness-based modeling to inform workflow improvements.
